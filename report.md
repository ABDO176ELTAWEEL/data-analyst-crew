# Report: Spending Score by Age Group

**Highest Spending Group**
The age group with the highest average spending score is the **18-30** category, which has an average spending score of **58.58**.

**Assumptions**
*   Age was segmented into four categories: 18-30, 31-45, 46-60, and 61+.
*   The mean was used to represent the average spending score for each group.
*   The binning method was adjusted to ensure all data points, including the minimum age of 18, were included in the calculation.

**Insight**
There is a clear downward trend in average spending as customers age; the 18-30 group leads with **58.58**, while the 46-60 group represents the lowest average spending at **35.18**. This suggests that younger customers are more active in their spending compared to their older counterparts within this dataset.
